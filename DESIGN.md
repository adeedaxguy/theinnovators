# The INNOVATORS Design System

## Direction

The interface is a dense visual innovation portal rather than a marketing site. It combines a black broadcast header, image-led feature board, persistent audience and sector navigation, and white editorial workspaces with strong blue and red signals.

## Color

- Ink: `#101820` for headings, rules, and high-contrast utility surfaces.
- Bright blue: `#049fe3` for primary actions, active controls, maps, and video play controls.
- Deep blue: `#0675ad` for labels and secondary emphasis.
- Red: `#e53b40` for live, ranking, urgency, and news signals.
- Soft blue: `#eef8fc` for discovery and intelligence panels.
- Neutral line: `#d9e2e8` for grids and editorial separation.
- White remains the dominant reading surface.

## Typography

- Spartan is the product typeface.
- Section headings are bold, uppercase, and paired with a strong black rule.
- Body text should stay at 15–18px in working areas.
- Compact labels may use 12–14px only when paired with clearly readable primary text.
- Hero type is reserved for the virtual showroom and primary media stage.

## Layout

- The platform header, ticker, billboard grid, sector menu, and audience rail remain shared across routes.
- Main desktop workspaces use a broad central canvas plus one or two narrower intelligence rails.
- Sections are unframed white bands; cards are reserved for repeated media, tools, and ranked items.
- Media is rectangular and editorial, with consistent height and visible play controls.
- At tablet and mobile widths, central media leads and side rails stack beneath it.

## Components

- **Showroom stage:** immersive image/video surface, live status, direct entrance action, and view selectors.
- **Video stage:** bright blue-and-white frame with a clear play action and contextual title.
- **Media rail:** horizontally scrollable editorial cards with stable media ratios.
- **Intelligence rail:** compact blue or neutral panel containing rankings, filters, watchlists, or AI tools.
- **World map:** pale blue ocean, bright blue active ecosystems, red selected state, and a white detail panel.
- **Encyclopedia tabs:** horizontal audience selector driving one focused intelligence panel at a time.

## Interaction

- Media selection updates the central video or opens the shared preview modal.
- Tabs, filters, map regions, showroom views, directory search, and AI prompts visibly update content.
- Buttons use familiar icons plus concise labels where the command is not universally understood.
- Focus states remain visible and controls use semantic buttons, inputs, labels, and navigation regions.

## Responsive Rules

- Desktop: multi-column editorial dashboard.
- Tablet: core stage plus one rail; secondary rails wrap below.
- Mobile: single-column order with the primary media stage first, horizontal rails for repeated media, and scrollable utility tabs.
- No page-level horizontal overflow; only explicit rails and menus may scroll horizontally.
