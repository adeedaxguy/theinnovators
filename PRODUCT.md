# The INNOVATORS Product Context

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Responsive web application built with Next.js.

## Users

Innovation ecosystem participants: startup founders, corporations, investors, universities, accelerators, industry associations, policy makers, service providers, country ecosystem teams, researchers, and innovation-focused audiences.

## Product Purpose

The INNOVATORS is a visual, video-first innovation intelligence platform. It combines editorial media, live and recorded company showrooms, company and ecosystem data, global discovery, rankings, watchlists, and AI-assisted research in one connected experience.

## Positioning

The product sits between an innovation newsroom, Crunchbase-style company intelligence, Dealroom-style ecosystem discovery, LinkedIn-style people and company networks, OpenAlex-style research context, and an interactive global map.

## Operating Context

Users scan dense information repeatedly, compare organizations and countries, watch briefings, save discoveries, and move between media and structured intelligence. The interface must prioritize legibility, fast scanning, and direct interaction over marketing-page composition.

## Capabilities And Constraints

- Preserve the existing platform header, billboard grid, sector menu, audience rail, and editorial visual language.
- New pages must work across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Video and showroom content uses preview media until production embeds and streams are supplied.
- Directory, ranking, watchlist, and AI results are demonstrative frontend data until production APIs are connected.
- Interactive controls must visibly update the relevant content and remain keyboard accessible.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: The INNOVATORS.
- Primary surfaces are white, black, bright cyan-blue, red accents, and restrained neutral grays.
- Typography follows a readable editorial scale comparable to established financial news sites.
- Page composition is dense, image-led, and modular, with square or lightly rounded frames.
- Canva pages 6 through 13 are the binding visual and structural reference for `/companyA`, `/AI`, and `/innovators`.

## Evidence On Hand

- Existing production application and established `/usa` and `/world` visual system.
- Canva design pages 6 through 13 reviewed in full.
- Existing local billboard and journey icon assets.
- Existing editorial, leader, video, and company imagery in the application data layer.

## Product Principles

1. Put the primary working surface in the first viewport.
2. Keep video, discovery, and intelligence connected through direct selection.
3. Prefer readable information density over oversized decoration.
4. Make audience and sector context persistent and easy to change.
5. Use consistent media ratios and strong section labels for scanning.

## Accessibility And Inclusion

- Maintain visible keyboard focus and semantic controls.
- Keep body text at a readable minimum size and avoid low-contrast text.
- Reflow complex columns into a logical single-column order on small screens.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences for decorative animation.
